## Brief

Run a persona reaction analysis on a proposed "deal risk dashboard" feature (a dashboard surfacing deals the AI scores as at-risk, with the behavioral signals behind the score).

## Expectations

- Covers all three personas (P-001, P-002, P-003) with a predicted reaction for each
- Each prediction grounded in that persona's documented product hooks, pains, or goals — cited
- References use case status honestly (UC-001 is the relevant use case; no claims beyond its status)
- Identifies whether the feature closes any documented gap, citing `use-cases/uc-XXX.md#gaps` explicitly if so
- Follows the Product persona-reaction format from AGENTS.md
- Zero forbidden language; no invented personas or capabilities
- Flags Brain gaps (e.g., undocumented persona attitude) rather than inventing reactions
