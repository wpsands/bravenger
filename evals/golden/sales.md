## Brief

Write an outbound email for a VP Sales at a 200-person B2B software company who just missed forecast two quarters in a row.

## Expectations

- Matches the prospect to P-001 (The VP Sales) and qualifies the company against ICP (200 employees is in range; says so or implicitly respects it)
- Anchors to a documented buying signal from `icp.md#buying-signals` (missed forecasts)
- Uses approved VP language from the messaging framework (VP2 is the natural anchor)
- 100–200 words per email (per AGENTS.md Sales format), personalized to the signal
- No capability promises beyond use case status fields; no forbidden language
- Factual claims cited `[Source: filename#section]` (citations may sit outside the email body as annotations)
- Does not disparage the prospect's current CRM vendor by name
